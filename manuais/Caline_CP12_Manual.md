# Caline CP-12 Pure Sky Overdrive Guitar Pedal Instruction Manual

## Introduction
The Caline CP-12 Pure Sky Overdrive is a versatile guitar effect pedal designed to deliver a range of tones from pristine clean boost to smooth, compressed overdrive. This pedal is engineered to preserve the original timbre of your amplifier while adding rich harmonic content.

## What's in the Box
* 1 Caline CP-12 Pure Sky OD Effect Pedal
* 1 Instruction Manual

## Key Features
* **Pure and Clean Overdrive:** Provides a highly pure and clean overdrive, sustaining the original amplifier timbre.
* **True Bypass:** Minimizes tone loss when the effect is disengaged.
* **Durable Construction:** Features a full metal case and a sturdy stomp switch for reliability.
* **Versatile Tone Control:** Volume and Gain controls allow for sounds ranging from clean boost to hard rock overdrive.
* **Power Requirement:** Operates with a negative center 9-Volt DC power supply (not included).

## Controls and Connections
The Caline CP-12 Pure Sky pedal features four control knobs and standard input/output jacks.
* **VOL (Volume):** Adjusts the overall output level of the effect.
* **GAIN:** Controls the amount of overdrive or distortion. Lower settings provide a clean boost or subtle grit, while higher settings introduce more pronounced overdriven tones.
* **TREBLE:** Adjusts the high-frequency response of the tone.
* **BASS:** Adjusts the low-frequency response of the tone.
* **IN (Input Jack):** Connect your guitar or previous effect pedal here.
* **OUT (Output Jack):** Connect to your amplifier or next effect pedal.
* **DC 9V (Power Input):** Connect a 9-Volt DC negative center power supply.
* **Footswitch:** Engages or disengages the effect. The LED indicator will light up when the effect is active.

## Setup
1. **Power Connection:** Connect a standard 9-Volt DC negative center power supply (not included) to the DC 9V input jack.
2. **Input Connection:** Connect your guitar to the 'IN' jack of the pedal using a standard 1/4-inch instrument cable.
3. **Output Connection:** Connect the 'OUT' jack of the pedal to your amplifier's input using a standard 1/4-inch instrument cable.
4. **Initial Settings:** Start with the 'VOL' and 'GAIN' knobs at their minimum positions, and 'TREBLE' and 'BASS' at the 12 o'clock (neutral) position.

## Operating Instructions
* **Engaging the Effect:** Press the footswitch to activate the overdrive effect. The LED indicator will light up.
* **Adjusting Gain:** Slowly increase the 'GAIN' knob to introduce overdrive. Start with lower settings for a clean boost or subtle grit, and increase for more pronounced overdriven tones.
* **Setting Volume:** Adjust the 'VOL' knob to achieve the desired output level, balancing it with your amplifier's clean tone.
* **Tone Shaping:** Use the 'TREBLE' and 'BASS' knobs to fine-tune the tonal characteristics. Turning clockwise increases the respective frequencies, while turning counter-clockwise decreases them.
* **Disengaging the Effect:** Press the footswitch again to bypass the effect. The LED indicator will turn off.

## Maintenance
* **Cleaning:** Use a soft, dry cloth to clean the pedal's exterior. Avoid abrasive cleaners or solvents.
* **Power Supply:** Always use a regulated 9-Volt DC negative center power supply to prevent damage.
* **Storage:** Store the pedal in a dry, cool environment away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures when not in use.
* **Cable Care:** Ensure all instrument cables are in good condition to prevent signal loss or noise.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No sound when pedal is active.** | Check power supply connection and ensure it's 9V DC negative center. Verify all cables are securely connected. |
| **Weak or distorted sound.** | Check guitar and amplifier volume settings. Ensure 'VOL' and 'GAIN' knobs are adjusted appropriately. Test with different cables. |
| **Excessive noise or hum.** | Use a high-quality, regulated power supply. Ensure proper grounding. Check for interference from other electronic devices. |
| **LED not lighting up.** | Verify power supply connection and functionality. The pedal may not be receiving power. |
